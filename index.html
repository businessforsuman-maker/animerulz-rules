Community Rules & Platform Guidelines

Welcome

Animerulz is built for anime lovers. To keep the platform safe, enjoyable, and respectful for everyone, all users must follow the rules below. Using this website means you agree to these guidelines.

General Rules

  • Respect all users. Harassment, hate speech, or abuse is not allowed.
  • No spamming, flooding, or repetitive content.
  • No impersonation of staff or other users.
  • No promotion of illegal activities.
  • Do not attempt to exploit bugs or vulnerabilities.

Comment Guidelines

  • Keep discussions related to anime.
  • No toxic behavior, threats, or personal attacks.
  • No NSFW, disturbing, or explicit content.
  • No advertising or self-promotion.
  • Spoilers must be respectful to other viewers.

Account Usage

  • Each user is responsible for their account activity.
  • Sharing accounts is discouraged.
  • Fake or automated accounts may be removed.
  • Usernames containing offensive terms will be changed or banned.

Content Policy

Users may not upload or distribute harmful material. This includes malware, phishing content, extreme violence, or anything intended to damage the platform or its community.

Copyright Disclaimer

Animerulz does not host media files directly. All content is provided by third-party sources. If you believe your copyrighted material appears here, appropriate action will be taken.

Rule Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent bans without prior notice. Severe abuse can lead to immediate termination of access.

Final Note

These rules exist to protect the community. By respecting them, you help keep Animerulz enjoyable for everyone.
